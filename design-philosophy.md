# Caribbean Gateway — Design Philosophy

A unified system for everything we put out: website, social, decks, brochures, emails.

---

## 1. Brand Positioning

**Caribbean Gateway is a small, on-the-ground consultancy that helps Caribbean students and international families enroll in affordable, accredited medical programs in Georgia.**

We are not an agency. We are a person — Caribbean-born, Tbilisi-based — who walks alongside families through a process most have never navigated before.

### Three things we always lead with
1. **Caribbean roots.** We share the culture. We get the family dynamics, the cost pressures, the dream.
2. **Georgian ground game.** We live in Tbilisi. We've walked into these universities. We meet you at the airport.
3. **Plain-spoken honesty.** No glossy promises. We tell you what works, what costs, what's hard.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone

### Voice (consistent everywhere)
- **Warm authority.** Knowledgeable older sibling, not slick consultant.
- **Direct.** Short sentences. Plain words. No "leverage," no "bespoke," no "world-class."
- **Specific.** Numbers, names, examples. "$4,000–$8,000 a year," not "affordable."
- **Caribbean cadence.** Subtle — never costume. We can say "we got you" once. Not on every page.

### Tone (varies by context)
| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Home hero | Confident, inviting |
| Services / Process | Plain, procedural, reassuring |
| About | Personal, story-led |
| FAQ | Patient, anticipates worry |
| Contact | Open, low-pressure |

### Words we use
*walk you through, on the ground, accredited, recognized, family, affordable, English-taught, Tbilisi, six years, USMLE, in person*

### Words we don't
*synergy, journey (overused), unlock, world-class, premium, exclusive, dream big, transform, empower, robust solutions*

### Copy rules
1. Write for one parent reading at the kitchen table.
2. Lead with the verb. "We meet you at the airport." Not "Airport pickup is among our services."
3. One idea per sentence. Two sentences per paragraph on the web.
4. Numbers beat adjectives. "$4,000/year" beats "affordable."
5. Name the worry before you answer it. ("Worried about the language? Here's the thing —")

## 3. Visual System

### Color
- **Ivory** `#f5f1e8` — primary background. Warm, paper-like.
- **Deep Navy** `#0f1f33` — primary text and dark surfaces. Trust.
- **Sage** `#7a8f7a` — secondary, supporting blocks, dividers.
- **Burnished Gold** `#b8924a` — accent only. Numerals, underlines, pull-quotes.
- **Clay** `#c47a5a` — sparing emoji-of-warmth, tags.

Rule: a page uses ivory + navy + ONE other. Never all five.

### Type
- **Display:** `Newsreader` (serif, optical-sized). Headlines, pull quotes.
- **UI / body:** `Inter Tight`. Nav, buttons, paragraphs.
- **Mono accent:** `JetBrains Mono`. Numerals, labels, image captions, "01 / 04" markers.

Headings are tight (`-0.02em`). Body is `1.65` line-height. Never go below 16px.

### Layout
- 12-column grid, 1280px max content, generous side gutters.
- Sections breathe — minimum 96px vertical between major blocks.
- Asymmetry over centering. Editorial feel, not corporate slide.

### Imagery
- Real photos > stock. When stock is needed: muted, documentary, one human in frame.
- **No** rounded-corner gradient cards. No emoji-as-icon. No 3D illustrations.
- Image placeholders are striped + labeled in mono ("portrait — Tbilisi office").

### Motion
- Tiny. Hover lifts 1–2px. Underlines draw on hover. No parallax. No scroll-jacking.

---

## 4. Marketing Application

Same system, every surface.

- **Instagram:** ivory backgrounds, single navy headline in Newsreader, a mono caption. One photo per post.
- **WhatsApp signature:** plain text, name + role + "Tbilisi, Georgia."
- **Email:** ivory or white, navy text, gold rule under the signature. Never "Best regards," — use "— Gabriel."
- **Brochures / PDFs:** match site type and color exactly. Page numbers in mono.

---

## 5. The "Caribbean Gateway sentence"

If we had to describe the brand in one line:

> **A Caribbean voice in Tbilisi, walking families through affordable medical school — one student at a time.**
